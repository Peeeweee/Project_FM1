    Connect FM1
    1. Connect your FM1 directly by USB and turn it on.
    2. Close other music editors that might be using it.
    3. Find its ports, then choose the FM1 input and output.
    MIDI carries notes and controls, not sound. To record the sound too, choose Audio in the studio. Chrome or Edge works best.
    

    Help & credits
    Notes or audio?
    Notes (MIDI) remember which keys you played and when. You can change their pitch and timing later. Audio records the actual sound. You can listen back, trim it, and save a WAV.
    What can the app see?
    Only what the FM1 sends over USB. Live notes and MIDI controls can be shown. Preset names and button presses depend on firmware support; missing information is marked as unavailable.
    Built with open-source work
    Three.js (MIT) powers the 3D sculpture. The pinned offline updater is adapted from SLOOP/Felucca (GPL-3.0). Firmware previews are credited in their detail views.
